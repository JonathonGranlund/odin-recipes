Mac and Cheese

Description:

This golden delicious treat is worth more than gold
Made simply with few ingredients it is the perfect meal.
Just elbow pasta and cheese and a couple of other ingredients
take you into the fold of italian cuisine. Anyone can make it.

Ingredients:

  • 2 boxes of your favorite brand of mac
  • Milk
  • Butter
  • Water

Steps to Make It:

  1. Boil water.
  2. Place noodles from both boxes in water.
  3. Boil noodles for 8 minutes.
  4. Strain noodles from water.
  5. Add a cup of milk.
  6. Add a stick of butter.
  7. Add both cheese packets.
  8. Stir until properly mixed.
  9. Enjoy!
