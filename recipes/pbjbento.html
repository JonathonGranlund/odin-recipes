PBJ Bento Box

Description:

The Japanese have gifted the world with all kinds of wonderful cultural things.
Here is where their beloved Bento Boxes meet western influence. I present the PBJ Bento Box.
This is more than a mere lunch box. It is a work of art.

The Japanese bento boxes share beauty and astetics with those who eat them.
In the picture above you can see a cute creature. Why shouldn't your food look fun?

Ingredients:

  • Peanut Butter
  • Jelly
  • Bread
  • Cookies
  • Fruits or Berries
  • Veggies

Steps to Make It:

  1. Choose a bento box container.
  2. Place several fruits or berries in one slot.
  3. Place several veggies in another slot.
  4. Make a PBJ Sandwhich.
  5. Cut your PBJ to desired design.
  6. Place PBJ in main slot.
  7. Fill remaining space of main slot with cookies.
  8. Enjoy at your leisure.
