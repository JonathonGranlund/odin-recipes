Hot Dogs

Description:

A meat filled casing on a bun. This is an American favorite.
Gifted to the US by German culture, this treat is
a must have at any baseball game.

Ingredients:

  • Buns
  • Hot Dogs
  • Water

How to Make It:

  1. Bring water to a boil.
  2. Boil Hot Dogs in water until they begin to roll.
  3. If they begin to split take them out anyway.
  4. Place the Hot Dog on a bun.
  5. Enjoy!
